Self-drive travel in Italy: preparing your Chinese driving licence

For Chinese-speaking travellers living overseas and planning to rent a car in Italy: check your original licence, applicable international driving permit or official Italian translation requirements, and the rental company’s own rules before departure.

01

Before you travel

Check the issuing country or region, licence validity, residence circumstances and rental company documentation requirements.

02

Prepare documents

03

On arrival

Pick up original paper documents at a confirmed collection location or by delivery, and carry the original driving licence for rental and driving.

Sworn translation is a formal Italian translation procedure, but it does not replace the original licence, grant driving rights automatically or guarantee car rental acceptance.

Foreign driving licence: sworn translation in Italy

Direct assistance for visitors and residents needing an Italian sworn translation of their driving documents.

  • Accurate translation directly from the source-language document, followed by the applicable sworn translation procedure.
  • Clear digital photographs can be provided for an initial assessment.
