DRIVING IN ITALY

Sworn Translation of Foreign Driving Licences in Milan

Studio TesoroMilano provides Chinese-to-Italian sworn driving licence translations, with collection in central Milan and selected airport handover or shipping by prior arrangement.

How the service works

  • A complete Italian translation made directly from the original Chinese-language driving licence.
  • Provide clear images initially for assessment of requirements and availability.
  • Timing, fees and delivery arrangements are confirmed before acceptance.
  • Collect near Milan Duomo or arrange selected airport handover or shipping.
  • Urgent processing may be available subject to working days and scheduling.

Driving eligibility and car hire

A sworn translation does not replace your original driving licence. Driving eligibility depends on the licence, residence status and applicable Italian road rules, including Article 135 where relevant. Rental company document checks may differ from legal road-use requirements.
