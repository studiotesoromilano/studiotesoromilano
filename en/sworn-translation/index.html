What is a sworn translation in Italy?

A sworn translation (traduzione giurata / asseverata) is accompanied by a translator’s formal oath under the applicable procedure. It does not authenticate the source document or guarantee acceptance by every authority.

Sworn translation versus apostille

An apostille generally certifies formal elements of certain public documents for cross-border use; it does not certify translation accuracy. Requirements and sequence vary according to the issuing country, destination and receiving institution.

How to request the service

  1. Describe the document, original language and approximate length.
  2. Tell us the receiving organisation, destination country and deadline.
  3. Receive confirmation of scope, quotation, timetable and delivery options.
  4. Arrange collection in Milan, shipping or a confirmed handover location.

Frequently asked questions

Does every foreign document require a sworn translation?

No. Requirements depend on the receiving authority and intended use.

Can a Chinese driving licence be translated using only English labels?

The original Chinese content should be translated directly and completely into Italian where applicable.

Is an apostille the same as sworn translation?

No. These are separate procedures with different purposes.

Is airport document collection available?

Selected locations may be available by prior agreement and confirmation.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the document and available appointments; standard driving licence orders should preferably be placed at least five working days in advance.

Does CTU registration automatically make a translation sworn?

No. The relevant oath procedure must be completed when required.
