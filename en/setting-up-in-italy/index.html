Choose the appropriate form of presence

Subsidiary

An Italian legal entity, commonly an S.r.l. or S.p.A., wholly or partly controlled by a foreign investor. Governance, capital and tax choices require case-specific professional review.

Branch

An extension of a foreign parent rather than a separate legal entity. Corporate resolutions, supporting evidence and registration requirements need to be reviewed.

Representative office

Generally limited to non-commercial activities such as liaison and market research. It is not interchangeable with an operating Italian company.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start planning while outside Italy?

Yes. Preparatory coordination may begin remotely. Signing by proxy and identity checks must be validated with the appointed notary for the specific case.

Must an S.r.l. always have €10,000 in capital?

The official Invest in Italy portal describes a standard €10,000 capital figure and circumstances allowing a lower amount, subject to applicable conditions and professional advice.

Does Studio TesoroMilano incorporate companies on its own?

No. We coordinate language and documents; reserved notarial, legal, tax and filing work is handled by qualified professionals.

Will foreign corporate documents need an Apostille?

It depends on the originating country, type of document, applicable treaties and the recipient’s requirements.
